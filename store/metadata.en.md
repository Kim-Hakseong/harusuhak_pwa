## Description (4000)
HaruSuhak ("a math problem a day") gives you one problem a day aligned to the achievement standards of Korea's 2015/2022 revised mathematics curriculum. No server, no data collected — every problem is generated on your device, and your progress stays on your device.

■ One problem a day, mapped to the curriculum
· Four tracks — Elementary (grades 4–6), Middle school, High school (Common Math, Algebra, Calculus I) and Everyday Math — with 56 representative problem types
· Each problem shows its achievement-standard code, unit, difficulty and reference material
· Everyone gets the same "problem of the day" on the same date

■ Unlimited variations
· One tap generates new numbers, conditions, choices and a full solution for the same type
· Wrong choices reflect real student mistakes: sign errors, reciprocal confusion, unit slips
· Every answer key has been checked by an independent verifier across 100 variations per type

■ Figures for geometry and graphs
· 13 problem types include a figure: trapezoids, circles, cuboids, right triangles, quadratic and cubic graphs, circles on the coordinate plane, internal division points
· The value you need to find is shown as "?" so the figure never gives the answer away

■ Step-by-step solutions and a mistake notebook
· See a step-by-step solution, concept summary and formula right after you answer
· Missed problems collect in your mistake notebook — retry them with new numbers
· Track your streak and weekly stamps

■ Daily reminder
· Get a reminder at the time you choose (8:00 AM by default). Notification permission is requested only when you turn it on

■ Privacy
· No account, no ads, no tracking, no network requests
· Progress is stored on your device only; export a JSON backup via the share sheet anytime

Based on the HaruSuhak web app by 정한결 and 정재상 (MIT License), released for iOS by TECHSENSE. The app content is in Korean.

## Review Notes
HaruSuhak is an offline math practice app with no login. There is no server: all content (problems, choices, solutions, figures) is generated on-device from a seed. No network requests, accounts, payments, ads or tracking.
How to review: (1) Solve today's problem on the Home tab and tap "정답 확인" (Check answer) to see the step-by-step solution. (2) "숫자 바꿔 변형 문제 풀기" generates a new variation. (3) The Curriculum tab lets you open any of the 56 types (e.g. MID-06 includes a figure). (4) Settings → "매일 알림" (Daily reminder) requests notification permission only when switched on. (5) Settings → JSON backup opens the iOS share sheet.
The app content is in Korean.
